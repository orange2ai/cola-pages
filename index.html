Why Clean X Up

You opened X for the content, not to be managed by the interface.

Clean X Up does not redesign X or decide what you should read. It simply turns off the little things that ask for your attention before you have chosen to give it.

Make it yours

One master switch. Four quiet decisions.

Each feature can be turned on or off independently. The popup follows your browser language, with English and Simplified Chinese included.
